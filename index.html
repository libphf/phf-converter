Pure High-Fidelity Format (PHF)

PHF is a lossless image codec designed for ultra-low latency and bit-exact preservation. It utilizes R-G/G/B-G decorrelation, adaptive JPEG-LS MED prediction or Palette mode, and order-1 context N-Huffman entropy coding with CRC-32 integrity validation.

100% Client-Side & Private

Every byte is compressed and decompressed inside your web browser using high-performance WebAssembly SIMD128. Your images are never sent to any external server.
